About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ConvertNova handles information when you visit our website and use our online file conversion and processing tools.

We aim to collect and use information only when it is necessary to operate, improve and secure our website and services.

01

Information Collection

ConvertNova may receive information that you voluntarily provide when using certain features of the website, such as contacting us through our contact form.

Depending on how you use the website, information you provide may include your name, email address, subject and message.

We do not require you to create an account simply to use the available file conversion tools.

02

Uploaded File Processing

Some ConvertNova tools allow you to upload files for conversion, compression, editing or other processing operations.

Uploaded files are used to perform the operation requested by you. We do not intentionally use uploaded files for unrelated purposes.

Files may be temporarily stored or processed on our servers when required to provide a particular tool. Files are intended to be removed after processing whenever technically possible.

You should avoid uploading files containing highly sensitive information unless the particular service is appropriate for that information.

03

Cookies and Similar Technologies

ConvertNova may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage and improve the user experience.

Cookies may also be used by third-party services operating on our website.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website.

04

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use ConvertNova, such as which pages are visited, how visitors interact with the website and how website performance can be improved.

Analytics information may be collected using cookies or similar technologies by the relevant service provider.

05

Advertising

ConvertNova may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertisements and to help determine which advertisements may be relevant to users.

Third-party advertising services operate according to their own privacy policies and practices.

If advertising preferences or consent controls are provided on the website, visitors can use those controls to manage their available choices.

06

Third-Party Services

ConvertNova may use third-party providers for services such as analytics, advertising, website infrastructure, security, email communication or other functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of third-party services when applicable.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information handled through ConvertNova.

However, no website, online service or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to support requests, maintaining website functionality, preventing abuse and meeting applicable requirements.

Uploaded files used by conversion tools are intended to be removed after processing whenever technically possible.

09

Children's Privacy

ConvertNova is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10

External Links

ConvertNova may contain links to external websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites operated by third parties. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

11

Your Choices

Depending on the services and features you use, you may have choices regarding cookies, advertising preferences and information submitted through the website.

You can also contact us if you have questions about information you have submitted through our website.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with the revised update date.

13

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled on ConvertNova, please contact us.
