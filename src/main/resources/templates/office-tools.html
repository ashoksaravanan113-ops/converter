OFFICE TIPS

Tips for Working With Office Documents

A few simple checks can help you get better results when converting office documents.

Keep the original file

Keep a copy of your original Word, Excel or PowerPoint document before converting it to another format.

Check the converted document

Open the converted file and review important text, tables, slides, page breaks and formatting before sharing it.

Use PDF for final sharing

A PDF can be useful when you want to share a document without expecting the recipient to edit the original office file.

Check before printing

Review page layout, tables, headings and slide content before printing a converted PDF document.
