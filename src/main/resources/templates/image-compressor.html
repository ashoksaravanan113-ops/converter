How to Compress Images

  1. Upload your images. Select JPG, PNG, or WEBP images from your device or drag the files into the upload area.
  2. Choose your compression settings. Select a compression level and adjust the quality slider to control the file size.
  3. Start the compression. Click the Compress Images button to process your files.
  4. Download your images. After the compression finishes, download the reduced-size image files.
