What Is a PDF to Image Converter?

A PDF to Image Converter turns PDF pages into common image formats such as JPG and PNG. Instead of sharing an entire PDF document, you can convert individual pages or a complete document into images for easier viewing, sharing, publishing, or editing.

PDF to image conversion is useful when you need a visual version of a document for a website, presentation, social media post, report, or other digital content.

Why Convert PDF to Images?

Converting PDF pages to images can be useful in many situations. Common reasons include:

  • Share individual PDF pages as images.
  • Extract charts, diagrams, illustrations, or other visual content.
  • Use document pages in presentations.
  • Publish PDF content on websites or social media.
  • Create image previews of PDF documents.
  • Make individual pages easier to view on image-based platforms.

JPG or PNG: Which Format Should You Choose?

The best image format depends on the type of content in your PDF and how you plan to use the converted images.

  • JPG: A good choice for photographs and documents where a smaller file size is important.
  • PNG: Better suited to graphics, illustrations, screenshots, and content where sharper details are important.
