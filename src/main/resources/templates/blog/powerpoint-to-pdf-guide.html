  • Provide a consistent document format for sharing.

What Happens When PowerPoint Is Converted to PDF?

During conversion, presentation slides are transformed into PDF pages. Text, images, charts, and other supported slide elements are reproduced in the resulting document according to the capabilities of the conversion process.

PowerPoint features that depend on presentation playback, such as animations and slide transitions, do not function as animations inside a standard PDF.
