Common Types of PDF Watermarks

Text Watermarks

Text watermarks can be used for labels such as "Confidential," "Draft," "Sample," or copyright notices. They are useful when the purpose of the watermark needs to be clearly visible.

Image Watermarks

Image watermarks can include company logos, signatures, icons, or other supported images. They are commonly used for branding and document identification.

Tips for Adding Better PDF Watermarks

  • Use a light or semi-transparent watermark so the document remains readable.
  • Avoid placing the watermark directly over important information.
  • Use a company logo when the goal is professional branding.
  • Keep the watermark consistent throughout the document.
  • Preview the final PDF before downloading or sharing it.
  • Keep the original PDF as a backup.
