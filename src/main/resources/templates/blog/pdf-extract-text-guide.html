What Is PDF Cropping?

PDF cropping changes the visible page area of a PDF document by removing unwanted margins, empty space, or areas that are not needed. It can help make documents cleaner and easier to read.

Cropping is useful for scanned documents, presentations, worksheets, reports, forms, and other PDF files where unnecessary page space needs to be removed.

Why Crop a PDF?

You may want to crop a PDF when pages contain excessive white space, unwanted borders, or content that distracts from the main document.

  • Remove unwanted page margins
  • Reduce unnecessary white space
  • Improve document presentation
  • Focus attention on important content
  • Prepare pages for printing or sharing

Benefits of Cropping PDF Pages

Tips for Cropping PDFs

  • Keep all important text inside the crop area.
  • Check every page before downloading.
  • Avoid cropping signatures or important page numbers.
  • Preview the result before sharing the document.
