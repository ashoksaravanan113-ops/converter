Tips for Better Word to PDF Conversion Results

A few simple checks before and after conversion can help ensure that your PDF looks the way you expect.

  • Review the Word document first. Check spelling, images, tables, and page breaks before conversion.
  • Use compatible fonts. Standard fonts can help reduce formatting differences between systems.
  • Check page layout. Review margins, spacing, headers, and footers before converting.
  • Review the converted PDF. Open the PDF and check its pages before sharing or submitting it.
  • Keep the original Word file. Save the DOC or DOCX version if you may need to make future edits.
