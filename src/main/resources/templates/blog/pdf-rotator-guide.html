What Is a PDF Rotator?

A PDF Rotator is an online tool that changes the orientation of PDF pages by rotating them 90°, 180°, or 270°. It can be useful for correcting scanned documents, fixing sideways pages, and improving document readability before sharing, printing, or archiving.

Why Rotate PDF Pages?

PDF pages can sometimes appear sideways or upside down after scanning or combining documents. Rotating the affected pages can make the document easier to read and present.

  • Fix incorrectly scanned PDF pages.
  • Correct sideways or upside-down pages.
  • Improve document readability.
  • Prepare PDFs for printing.
  • Create cleaner and more professional documents.
