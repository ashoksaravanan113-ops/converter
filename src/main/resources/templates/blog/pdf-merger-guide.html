What Is a PDF Merger?

A PDF merger is a tool that combines two or more PDF files into a single document. Instead of managing several separate PDF files, you can combine them into one organized document that is easier to share, store, print, or upload.

PDF merging can be useful when you need to combine reports, invoices, assignments, applications, presentations, scanned documents, or other related PDF files.

Why Merge PDF Files?

Combining related PDF files can make document management simpler and reduce the need to send or store multiple files separately.

  • Combine multiple PDF documents into one file.
  • Share one document instead of several attachments.
  • Keep related reports and projects organized.
  • Prepare multiple documents for printing.
  • Make document uploads easier to manage.

Tips for Better PDF Merging Results

  • Arrange your PDF files in the correct order before merging.
  • Check that you selected all required documents.
