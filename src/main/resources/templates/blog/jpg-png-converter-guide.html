Tips for Better Image to PDF Conversion

A few simple steps can help you create cleaner and more useful PDF documents from your images.

  1. Use high-resolution images. Higher-quality source images can help produce clearer PDF pages.
  2. Arrange images before converting. Check the order of your images so the PDF pages appear in the correct sequence.
  3. Check image orientation. Make sure portrait and landscape images are positioned correctly before conversion.
  4. Crop unnecessary borders. Remove unwanted areas around an image when they are not needed.
  5. Review the final PDF. Open the converted document and check the pages before sharing or printing it.
