What Is PDF Page Numbering?

PDF page numbering is the process of adding visible numbers to the pages of a PDF document. Page numbers help readers identify individual pages and navigate through longer documents more easily.

Numbered PDF pages are useful for reports, assignments, books, manuals, contracts, invoices, presentations, research papers, and other multi-page documents.

Why Add Page Numbers to a PDF?

Adding page numbers makes longer PDF documents easier to reference, organize, and navigate.

  • Make multi-page documents easier to navigate.
  • Help readers quickly identify specific pages.
  • Make reports and assignments easier to reference.
  • Improve the organization of printed documents.
  • Give professional documents a consistent structure.
  • Make page references easier during meetings, reviews, and discussions.
