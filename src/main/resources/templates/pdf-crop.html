How to Crop PDF Pages

  1. Upload your PDF files. Select PDF files from your device or drag them into the upload area.
  2. Choose the crop amount. Select a small, medium, or large crop based on how much unwanted page area you want to remove.
  3. Choose where to apply the crop. Apply the selected crop to all pages or to the first page only.
  4. Crop and download. Click Crop PDF, preview the cropped file, and download it when processing is complete.
