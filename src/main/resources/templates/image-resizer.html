How to Resize an Image

  1. Upload your image. Select a JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP image from your device or drag the image into the upload area.
  2. Choose your dimensions. Enter the required width and height, and keep Maintain Aspect Ratio enabled if needed.
  3. Start resizing. Click the Resize Images button to process your image.
  4. Download your resized image. After resizing is complete, preview and download the generated image.
